# Hunter Research 001

## Title

Identifying Market Regime Using Trinity

## Source

Glitch YouTube Transcript

## Objective

Understand how institutional traders identify market regime before selecting a trading strategy.

---

## Executive Summary

The transcript demonstrates that institutional traders first determine the current market regime before evaluating trade opportunities.

Rather than immediately searching for bullish or bearish setups, the process begins by identifying whether the market is trending, chopping, whipsawing, or operating in a high-uncertainty environment.

This regime determines which trading strategies possess statistical edge and which should be avoided.

The presentation also emphasizes cross-market confirmation between SPX, SPY, and QQQ, institutional node evolution, velocity, and contextual reasoning rather than isolated signal interpretation.

---

## Major Findings

- Market Regime should be identified before Pattern Recognition.
- Strategy changes depending upon regime.
- Trinity should be interpreted as one system.
- Velocity represents new institutional participation.
- Context is more valuable than individual nodes.
- Eliminate low-probability outcomes before selecting high-probability trades.
- Institutional positioning evolves continuously throughout the session.
